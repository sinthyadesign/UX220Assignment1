In my first five years after graduation, I really want to become financially independent. That means making enough money to support myself, building up my savings, and actually getting good at handling my finances.

I’d like to buy my own car, too. It would make life a lot easier—especially when it comes to work or just getting around. Mostly, I want to cover my own expenses without help, keep moving toward bigger financial goals, and just feel more in control of my future.
