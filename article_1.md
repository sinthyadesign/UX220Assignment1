Family

In five years, I want to look back and tell my classmates that I never lost sight of my family, even as I worked on my own goals. I hope I’ve filled these years with celebrations, trips, and just simple moments together building memories that really matter.

I also want to become more independent, not just for myself but so I can help out my family whenever they need it. If I manage to build a successful life and keep my relationships with them strong, that’s something I’ll truly be proud of.
