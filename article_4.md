# Professional

Five years from now, I want to be able to tell my classmates that I jumped right into my career after graduating from Laurier Brantford with a degree in UX Design. By then, I’ll have real projects under my belt and a portfolio I’m actually proud to show off.

I’m working on sharpening my skills—UX research, wireframing, prototyping, usability testing, all of it. I also want to be someone who speaks up in meetings and collaborates easily with others. Being able to look back at where I started in university and see how far I've come professionally, that’s something I’d be really proud of.
