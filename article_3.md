Five years from now, I want to look back and tell my classmates that I didn’t lose touch with the things that make me happy outside of work. I hope I’ve gone on adventures, visited places I haven't seen before, and made real memories with my friends and family. I want to keep exploring what creativity means to me.

I also want to see my small business, Decor Blossom, reach more people. Whether I’m making flower bouquets or custom blinged-out bottles, I love seeing someone smile because of something I made. My goal is for Decor Blossom to grow, but I don’t want it to turn into just another job—I want it to stay something I genuinely love doing in my spare time.
